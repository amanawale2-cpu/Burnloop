Fork + Spoon + Alert — 5 Variations

Five compositions, one required to be a crossed fork+spoon with alert, in gradient

24 — CROSSED, GRADIENT (AS REQUESTED)

Crossed Fork & Spoon

Classic crossed-cutlery pose, purple gradient fill, alert badge sitting right at the intersection.

25 — SIDE BY SIDE, FLAT

Standing Together

Fork and spoon upright side by side, flat single-color fill, red alert-triangle badge at the corner.

26 — NEGATIVE-SPACE CUTOUT

Cutlery on a Plate

Fork and spoon cut as negative space from a solid gradient plate, small red alert dot at the corner.

27 — CONTAINED IN THE ALERT SIGN

Cutlery Inside the Triangle

The alert triangle itself is the dominant shape (amber-to-red gradient); fork, spoon, and a dot are cut into it.

28 — MONOLINE, QUIET

Thin Crossed Outline

Same crossed pose as #24 but as thin outline strokes with a small solid dot accent — the quiet alternative.
