ConditionWise — Round 2

Six more icon directions, different pattern territory than round 1

07 — PURE GEOMETRIC

Nested Arcs

Two open arcs, offset and unequal weight, each with its own gap. Layers of attention around you rather than one closed loop. Quiet, a little more abstract than round 1.

08 — ROUNDED-RECT GRID

Selected Path

A loose grid of soft tiles, most faded, one fully solid — your specific path picked out of many possible ones. Reads as "personalized," not generic nutrition-app grid icons.

09 — LINE SYSTEM

Found Path

A route that dips, then rises cleanly to a solid point — the moment of finding the right answer. Honest flag: the shape reads close to a checkmark, which is common "done/verified" iconography — worth deciding if that's a feature (instantly legible) or a cliché to avoid.

10 — DOTS + GEOMETRY

Data Badge

A soft rounded-square badge (trust, credential) holding an asymmetric handful of points (your data: preference, history, condition, body stats). Warmer and more "product" than Compass Check.

11 — BOOLEAN OVERLAP

Overlap Focus

Two unequal circles (you + guidance) with their overlap tinted. Represents personalization as intersection rather than a one-way alert. Risk: overlap/Venn marks are a common motif in "collaboration" logos generally, so it reads less distinctly ownable.

12 — LAYERED RINGS

Meeting Point

Concentric rings (your whole health picture, layer by layer) with one accent point sitting off-center near the outer ring — meeting you where you actually are, not at the idealized center.
