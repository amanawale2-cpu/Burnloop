ConditionWise

Six icon directions — no flame, no fitness cliches, per the brand brief

01 — PURE GEOMETRIC

Guided Shield

A single shield silhouette with one rounded aperture cut into it, off-center. Protection with a point of focus — not a medical cross, not armor. Reads clearly at 16px.

02 — DOT MATRIX

Personal Constellation

Small points (your inputs — preference, history, condition, body stats) converging on one larger center point: you, understood. Asymmetric, not a generic grid.

03 — LINE SYSTEM

Settling Line

One continuous curve — a little uneven, then calm — resolving into a solid point. Deliberately not an ECG spike pattern; it's the feeling of finding steady ground, not a hospital monitor.

04 — DOTS + GEOMETRY

Compass Check

A ring (your whole health picture), a true-north tick, and an off-center point being gently guided toward balance. Guidance without a literal compass icon.

05 — LINES + GEOMETRY ⭐

Open Shield

An open ring with a gap at the top, and a single point resting exactly in that gap. "Your doctor can't watch every meal, we can" — visualized without saying it. My top pick: simplest, most ownable, most on-strategy.

06 — NODE NETWORK

Care Network

A central "you" node linked to three inputs at different weights and distances. The most literal "personalized guidance" story, at the cost of being the most complex mark of the six.
