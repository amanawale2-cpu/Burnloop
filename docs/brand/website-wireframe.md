# Website wireframe reference (long-form landing page structure)

A generic StoryBrand-style marketing landing page wireframe, provided as a
structural reference for the Burnloop marketing site's information architecture.
All copy shown is placeholder/template text — content should be replaced with
BurnLoop-specific messaging (see `docs/marketing/` and `docs/marketing/positioning-notes.md`
for the actual value props to slot in).

## Section-by-section structure

1. **Header** — logo (left), phone number ("333-333-3333"), CTA button (right).

2. **Hero ("Social Proof #1" eyebrow)**
   - H1: dream outcome / "what's in it for me" framing — the "stop doing X / no
     more Y / pain point" pattern.
   - Subtext bridging the old broken approach to the new one.
   - Primary CTA + a small friction-reducing line under it (reduce fear,
     uncertainty, doubt).
   - Right side: hero image/video/form that reinforces the value proposition.

3. **Social Proof #2** — "Stats, Testimonials, Case Studies, etc." heading, a
   3-up grid of social-proof cards, CTA button.

4. **Largest pain point** — headline naming the biggest problem solved and the
   "old way" of doing it, tied to BurnLoop's unique benefit; body copy bridges
   old solutions to the new one; CTA button.

5. **How it works** — headline framed to make success feel likely and
   effortless; a 3-up grid of numbered/step cards (headline + body each); CTA
   button. (Natural fit: the 5-step user journey from the marketing brief,
   trimmed to 3 headline steps — e.g. Scan → Get Alert → Take Action.)

6. **Value prop / big benefit #1–#3 ("WIIFM")** — three alternating
   image-left/text-right and text-left/image-right rows, each with a "dream
   outcome summary" eyebrow, a benefit headline, supporting body copy, and a
   CTA. (Natural fit: condition-aware alerts, exercise-to-offset, progress
   tracking — the three pillars from the marketing brief.)

7. **Differentiators** — "benefit-driven" headline (how BurnLoop does it +
   what makes it different), a 2×3 grid of 6 differentiator cards
   (headline + body), CTA button.

8. **FAQ** — "Social Proof #2" eyebrow repeated, "Frequently Asked Questions"
   heading, accordion list (6 questions shown as placeholders).

9. **Final recap / urgency** — "Social Proof #1" eyebrow, recap headline
   creating urgency, body copy, a 3-item checklist recapping the three value
   props, CTA + friction-reducing line, hero-style image/video on the right.

10. **Footer.**

## Notes for BurnLoop's version

- Brand color for CTAs/accents throughout the wireframe is the primary purple
  (`#7259c5` "Normal" from `docs/brand/figma-brand-guidelines.md`).
- Per `docs/marketing/positioning-notes.md`, the hero and pain-point sections
  should lead with condition-aware AI coaching, not calorie tracking.
- The "How it works" 3-step grid and the "scan flow" screenshots in
  `docs/brand/figma-app-flows.md` should reuse the same steps/language for
  consistency between marketing site and product.
