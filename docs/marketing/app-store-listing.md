**Title:** BurnLoop AI Health Coach for Meals

BurnLoop AI is a health coach that helps users make better dietary decisions in real
time. It scans food using AI, provides instant nutrition estimates, and offers
medical-based recommendations to prevent health risks related to diet. The app aims
to transform food decisions into data-driven health decisions, making it easier for
users to manage their diet daily without medical context. BurnLoop provides instant
AI-based food guidance at the moment of eating decision, helping users snap their
meal for instant nutrition estimates and burn calories with guided workouts. The
app's features include AI Meal Planner, Smart Coach, calorie tracking, and health
metrics integration. It is designed to be fast, friendly, actionable, personal,
motivating, and private, ensuring that users stay on track with their health goals.
