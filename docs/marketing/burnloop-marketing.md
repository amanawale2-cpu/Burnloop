# Burnloop Marketing

**What BurnLoop Is**

BurnLoop is an **AI-powered fitness coach** — not just another calorie counter. Its core differentiator is that it knows your health condition *before* giving you dietary advice. 

---

## **The Problem It Solves**

Most diet apps are generic. They don't account for the fact that someone with diabetes, pre-diabetes, kidney disease, or heart problems has very different — and medically critical — dietary needs. Poor diet compliance in these populations contributes to an estimated $237 billion in annual US healthcare costs. BurnLoop sits at the intersection of nutrition tracking and medical context.

---

## **The User Journey (5 Steps)**

**1\. Profile Setup** — Users enter body basics (age, weight, height, gender), select a health goal (Lose Weight, Manage Diabetes, Heart Health, etc.), and input their health condition (Diabetes, Pre-diabetes, High BP, Kidney Disease, PCOS, IBS, and more). BurnLoop then calculates BMI, sets personalized calorie targets, and generates a BurnLoop Progress showing exactly how it will monitor for you. 

**2\. Meal Scanning (Photo AI)** — The app's centerpiece. Users photograph any meal — no barcode needed — and instantly receive a full nutritional breakdown: calories, protein, carbs, sodium, vitamins. Critically, BurnLoop also delivers an **AI Health Alert** if the meal is problematic for the user's condition. For example, a pizza photo triggers a "Use Caution for Manage Diabetes" alert, flagging 1,660 kcal, 212g carbs, and 1,855mg sodium, with specific next steps like eating a smaller portion or choosing lean protein instead. This can be used *before* eating to make a smarter choice.

**3.Diet Recommendations** — Based on the profile, BurnLoop suggests a personalized daily calorie target (e.g., \~2,665 kcal/day for the test user) and pays close attention to carb load, sugar spikes, and sodium — calibrated to the user's specific condition.

**4\. Exercise to Offset** — After a meal, BurnLoop recommends specific exercises to burn the excess calories. Users tap into a workout timer (Walking, for example), which tracks elapsed time and calories burned in real time against a target (e.g., 2/720 cal). The burned calories are reflected in the home screen's progress ring alongside consumed calories.

**5\. Progress Tracking** — A central calorie ring on the home screen shows daily consumed vs. burned vs. the target. The Progress section offers Day/Week/Month/Year charts for calories consumed and burned, plus macros like protein and carbs, with week-over-week comparisons.
