# Positioning notes

- **Do not position BurnLoop as a calorie-tracking app.** Calorie tracking and the
  burn/offset workout loop are one feature, not the headline. Lead with condition-aware
  AI health coaching (diabetes, pre-diabetes, kidney disease, heart health, etc.) and
  the meal-scan safety alert as the core value, not calorie counting.
- When drafting site copy/IA, treat calories/macros as supporting detail under the
  broader "AI health coach that knows your condition" story — not the primary hook.
