# Brand visual notes (from ad creative in images/)

Observations from the six BurnLoop ad mockups (`images/image1.png`–`image6.png`),
useful as a starting visual reference for the website.

## Logo
- Wordmark "BurnLoop" (one word, capital B and L) set next to a small flame/droplet
  glyph mark in magenta/pink.
- Dark-background variants use a white/light wordmark; light-background variants use
  a near-black wordmark. The glyph mark stays magenta/pink in both.

## Color palette
- Dark surface: deep near-black plum/navy (~#150B1F–#1A0E28 range).
- Light surface: soft lavender/lilac (~#EDE7F5 range).
- Accent / brand color: vivid magenta-purple (~#B347F0–#D946EF range), used for
  headline highlight words, pill badges, and primary buttons on dark backgrounds.
- Primary button on light backgrounds: near-black, with the magenta reserved for the
  dark-background version.
- Decorative soft circular "blob" shapes in a translucent tint of the accent color,
  placed top-right and bottom-right of each card.

## Typography & layout
- Bold, tight-tracking sans-serif headlines (2–3 lines), large size, high contrast
  against the background.
- One highlighted word/phrase per headline set in the accent color (e.g. "Eat This.",
  "We Can.", "Start Today.").
- Small uppercase pill/badge label above the headline for context tagging
  (e.g. "AI HEALTH ALERT", "HEART HEALTH", "KIDNEY DISEASE", "PRE-DIABETES",
  "CONDITION-AWARE", "MANAGE CONDITION").
- Short one-sentence supporting copy under the headline.
- Pill-shaped primary CTA button ("Download BurnLoop Free", "Get the App Free",
  "Download & Scan Free", etc.) plus small secondary App Store / Google Play buttons.

## Messaging pattern (condition-specific hooks)
Each creative targets a specific condition/angle with a fear-then-relief headline
structure ("Your Blood Sugar Spikes When You Eat This." / "BurnLoop warns you...").
Conditions covered: general/all conditions, blood sugar/diabetes, heart health,
kidney disease (CKD), pre-diabetes.
