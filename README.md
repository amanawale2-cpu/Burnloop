# Burnloop
BurnLoop is an AI-powered fitness coach — not just another calorie counter. Its core differentiator is that it knows your health condition before giving you dietary advice. BurnLoop sits at the intersection of nutrition tracking and medical context.

---

## Design System — Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| `blDeepPurple` | `#3B1D8A` | Headers, banners, snapshot cards |
