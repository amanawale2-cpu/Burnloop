Frequently asked questions

Does BurnLoop provide medical advice?

No. BurnLoop provides general wellness and educational meal guidance based on details you choose to share. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a qualified healthcare professional.

How does meal scanning work?

You can scan or upload a meal photo, review detected foods, and receive nutrition-related context with practical next-step ideas.

Can BurnLoop help with allergies?

BurnLoop can incorporate stated allergies into meal-planning guidance. Always verify ingredients and follow advice from your allergy-care team.

Who is BurnLoop for?

It is for adults looking for a more personalized, practical way to plan meals and build healthier daily habits.

Important: BurnLoop is for general wellness and lifestyle support only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For medical questions or changes to a treatment plan, consult a qualified clinician.
