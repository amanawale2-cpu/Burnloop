What does diabetes-aware meal guidance mean?

Diabetes-aware meal guidance means using your stated goals and preferences to make meal planning more intentional. BurnLoop can provide nutrition-related context and practical meal ideas, but it cannot diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace individualized clinical advice.

Questions to discuss with your care team

A qualified clinician or registered dietitian can help you determine what is appropriate for your individual needs.

Targets

Ask which nutrition targets and meal patterns are appropriate for you.

Medication context

Discuss how meal timing and any medication plan should work together.

Personal changes

Ask when changes in symptoms or results should be reviewed professionally.

Helpful, not a substitute for care.

BurnLoop provides general wellness information only. If you use insulin, take glucose-affecting medication, are pregnant, have symptoms, or need individualized nutrition advice, consult your qualified healthcare team before making changes.
