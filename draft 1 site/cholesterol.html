What is cholesterol-friendly meal planning?

Cholesterol-friendly meal planning is the practice of choosing eating patterns that support heart-health goals discussed with a qualified clinician. BurnLoop can provide meal-level context and future-meal ideas, but it does not prescribe care or determine what is medically appropriate for you.
